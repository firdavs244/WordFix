1. LOYIHA HAQIDA UMUMIY MA'LUMOT
================================================================================

WordFix — bu sun'iy intellekt (AI) yordamida ingliz tili lug'atini
o'rganish uchun mo'ljallangan zamonaviy web platforma. Platforma
foydalanuvchilarga so'zlarni qo'shish, AI yordamida boyitish,
flashcard ko'rib chiqish (SM-2 algoritmi), testlar, o'yinlar,
AI chat va boshqa interaktiv usullar orqali so'z boyligini oshirish
imkonini beradi.

ASOSIY XUSUSIYATLAR:
  ✦ So'z boshqaruvi — CRUD, kategoriyalar, AI boyitish
  ✦ Spaced Repetition — SM-2 algoritmi bilan aqlli takrorlash
  ✦ AI boyitish — tarjima, ta'rif, misollar, sinonimlar, mnemonic
  ✦ TTS — Google Text-to-Speech (so'zlarni eshitish)
  ✦ Smart Import — matndan so'zlarni AI bilan tahlil qilish
  ✦ Testlar — 4 turdagi AI generatsiya qilingan testlar
  ✦ O'yinlar — 5 ta interaktiv o'yin (Speed Round, Word Match, va h.k.)
  ✦ AI Chat — sun'iy intellekt bilan ingliz tilida suhbat
  ✦ Confusing Pairs — chalkash so'zlarni aniqlash va mashq qilish
  ✦ Daily Challenges — kunlik topshiriqlar va bonuslar
  ✦ Onboarding — daraja aniqlash testi (A1–C2)
  ✦ Analitika — batafsil statistika, kalendar, progress
  ✦ Gamifikatsiya — XP, darajalar, 20 ta badge, streak
  ✦ Bildirishnomalar — eslatmalar, streak ogohlantirish, haftalik hisobot
  ✦ Dark/Light tema — 3 ta rejim (light, dark, system)

ARXITEKTURA:
  Backend:  Django 5.1 + DRF (Clean Architecture)
  Frontend: React 19 + TypeScript + Vite
  Database: PostgreSQL 16
  Cache:    Redis 7
  Queue:    Celery + Redis
  AI:       Groq (Llama 3.3) / OpenAI (GPT-4o-mini)
  TTS:      Google TTS (bepul)
  Deploy:   Docker + Docker Compose

6. FOYDALANUVCHI QO'LLANMASI — ASOSIY FUNKSIYALAR
================================================================================

--- 6.1 RO'YXATDAN O'TISH VA KIRISH ---

  1. http://localhost:5173/welcome sahifasiga o'ting
  2. "Ro'yxatdan o'tish" tugmasini bosing
  3. 3 bosqichli formani to'ldiring:
     - Email, username, parol (kamida 8 belgi, 1 raqam)
     - Ism, familiya, ona tili, o'rganish tili, daraja
     - Ma'lumotlarni tasdiqlang
  4. Onboarding (daraja aniqlash) testidan o'ting yoki o'tkazib yuboring
  5. Dashboard sahifasiga yo'naltirilasiz

--- 6.2 SO'Z QO'SHISH ---

  Usul 1 — Qo'lda qo'shish:
    1. "Words" sahifasiga o'ting (/words)
    2. "+ So'z qo'shish" tugmasini bosing
    3. So'z, tarjima kiriting (qolgan maydonlar ixtiyoriy)
    4. "Saqlash" tugmasini bosing
    5. AI avtomatik ravishda so'zni boyitadi (tarjima, ta'rif, misollar,
       sinonimlar, antonimlar, mnemonic, audio va h.k.)

  Usul 2 — Bulk qo'shish:
    1. "Words" sahifasida "Ko'p qo'shish" tugmasini bosing
    2. Har bir qatorda bitta so'z kiriting
    3. "Qo'shish" tugmasini bosing

  Usul 3 — Smart Import:
    1. "Import" sahifasiga o'ting (/import)
    2. Ingliz tilidagi matnni textarea ga yozing yoki joylang
    3. "Tahlil qilish" tugmasini bosing (AI matnni tahlil qiladi)
    4. AI topgan so'zlarni ko'rib chiqing (ishonch darajasi ko'rsatiladi)
    5. Kerakli so'zlarni tanlang va "Import qilish" tugmasini bosing

--- 6.3 SO'ZLARNI KO'RIB CHIQISH (REVIEW) ---

  1. "Review" sahifasiga o'ting (/review)
  2. Sessiya turini tanlang (Review/Learn/Mixed)
  3. Flashcard ko'rsatiladi:
     - Old tomon: so'z + talaffuz
     - Audio tugma bilan eshiting
     - Kartani aylantiring → tarjima, ta'rif, misollar ko'rsatiladi
  4. Sifat bahosi bering (0-5):
     0=Again, 1=Hard, 2=Difficult, 3=Good, 4=Easy, 5=Perfect
  5. SM-2 algoritmi asosida keyingi ko'rib chiqish vaqti hisoblanadi
  6. Sessiyani tugating → natijalarni ko'ring (XP, aniqlik, badge)
  7. Confetti animatsiya! 🎉

--- 6.4 TESTLAR ---

  1. "Tests" sahifasiga o'ting (/tests)
  2. Test turini tanlang:
     - Multiple Choice — 4 variantdan to'g'risini tanlash
     - Fill in the Blank — bo'sh joyni to'ldirish
     - Context Guess — kontekstdan so'zni topish
     - Mixed — aralash
  3. Qiyinlik va savol sonini tanlang (5-30)
  4. "Test boshlash" tugmasini bosing
  5. Har bir savolga javob bering (feedback darhol ko'rsatiladi)
  6. Natijalarni ko'ring (harf bahosi A-F, XP, savol ko'rib chiqish)

--- 6.5 O'YINLAR ---

  1. "Games" sahifasiga o'ting (/games)
  2. O'yin turini tanlang:

     Speed Round 🏃:
       - 60 soniya ichida imkon qadar ko'p so'z tarjima qiling
       - 4 variantdan to'g'risini tanlang
       - 80%+ aniqlik = 2x XP bonus

     Word Match 🔗:
       - So'z va tarjimani juftlang
       - Chap ustundagi so'zni, o'ng ustundagi tarjimani tanlang
       - Barcha juftliklar topilganda o'yin tugaydi

     Word Context 📖:
       - Paragraf o'qing, bo'sh joyga to'g'ri so'zni tanlang
       - 4 variantdan to'g'risini toping

     Story Builder 📝:
       - AI hikoya boshlanishini yozadi
       - Siz davom ettirasiz (ingliz tilida)
       - AI grammatikangizni baholaydi va tuzatishlar beradi

     Listening Challenge 🎧:
       - So'zni eshiting (audio)
       - Eshitganingizni yozing
       - Hint tugma = birinchi harf ko'rsatiladi

--- 6.6 AI CHAT ---

  1. "Chat" sahifasiga o'ting (/chat)
  2. Mavzu tanlang (Sayohat, Texnologiya, va h.k.) yoki o'zingiz yozing
  3. AI bilan ingliz tilida suhbatlashing
  4. AI sizning xatolaringizni tuzatadi:
     - Asl → Tuzatilgan (qizil → yashil) + tushuntirish
  5. Maqsadli so'zlaringiz suhbatda ishlatilishi kuzatiladi
  6. Suhbatni tugatganda XP olasiz

--- 6.7 CONFUSING PAIRS (CHALKASH SO'ZLAR) ---

  1. "Confusing Pairs" sahifasiga o'ting (/confusing-pairs)
  2. Testlar va review davomida chalkashtirilgan so'zlar avtomatik
     aniqlanadi
  3. Har bir juftlik uchun:
     - "Mashq" → AI maxsus mashq generatsiya qiladi
     - "Hal qilish" → yetarli mashq qilgandan so'ng belgilang

--- 6.8 DAILY CHALLENGES (KUNLIK TOPSHIRIQLAR) ---

  1. Dashboard sahifasida kunlik topshiriqlar ko'rsatiladi
  2. Har kuni yangi topshiriqlar (masalan: 5 ta so'z ko'rib chiqish,
     1 ta test o'tkazish, va h.k.)
  3. Barcha topshiriqlar bajarilganda bonus XP oling

--- 6.9 ANALITIKA ---

  1. "Analytics" sahifasiga o'ting (/analytics)
  2. Ko'rsatkichlarni ko'ring:
     - Umumiy statistika (so'zlar, testlar, o'yinlar, aniqlik)
     - Haftalik/oylik progress grafigi
     - O'qish kalendari (GitHub uslubidagi heatmap)
     - Eng qiyin so'zlar ro'yxati
     - So'z progress taqsimoti

--- 6.10 PROFIL ---

  1. "Profile" sahifasiga o'ting (/profile)
  2. Tahrirlash rejimiga o'ting:
     - Ism, bio, tillar, daraja, kunlik maqsad, timezone
  3. Parol almashtirish (eski parol + yangi parol)
  4. Avatar yuklash

9. API BILAN ISHLASH (Request/Response Namunalari)
================================================================================

STANDART JAVOB FORMATI (barcha API uchun):
  {
    "success": true,
    "data": { ... },
    "message": "Muvaffaqiyat",
    "errors": null,
    "meta": {
      "page": 1,
      "total_pages": 5,
      "total_count": 100,
      "page_size": 20
    }
  }

--- Ro'yxatdan o'tish ---
  POST http://localhost:8000/api/v1/auth/register/
  Body:
    {
      "email": "test@example.com",
      "username": "testuser",
      "password": "Test1234!",
      "password_confirm": "Test1234!",
      "full_name": "Test User"
    }
  Response:
    {
      "success": true,
      "data": {
        "user": { "id": "...", "email": "test@example.com", ... },
        "tokens": { "access": "eyJ...", "refresh": "eyJ..." }
      }
    }

--- Kirish ---
  POST http://localhost:8000/api/v1/auth/login/
  Body:
    {
      "email": "test@example.com",
      "password": "Test1234!"
    }
  Response:
    {
      "success": true,
      "data": {
        "user": { ... },
        "tokens": { "access": "eyJ...", "refresh": "eyJ..." }
      }
    }

--- So'z qo'shish ---
  POST http://localhost:8000/api/v1/words/
  Headers: Authorization: Bearer <access_token>
  Body:
    {
      "original_word": "serendipity",
      "translation": "kutilmagan yoqimli kashfiyot"
    }
  Response:
    {
      "success": true,
      "data": {
        "id": "uuid...",
        "original_word": "serendipity",
        "translation": "kutilmagan yoqimli kashfiyot",
        "enrichment_status": "pending",
        ...
      }
    }

--- So'zlar ro'yxati ---
  GET http://localhost:8000/api/v1/words/?page=1&search=hello&difficulty_level=easy
  Headers: Authorization: Bearer <access_token>
  Response:
    {
      "success": true,
      "data": [ { "id": "...", "original_word": "hello", ... } ],
      "meta": { "page": 1, "total_count": 42, ... }
    }

--- Health check ---
  GET http://localhost:8000/api/v1/health/
  Response:
    {
      "success": true,
      "data": { "status": "healthy", "database": "ok", "cache": "ok" }
    }

SWAGGER UI:
  http://localhost:8000/api/docs/ — barcha API endpointlarni interaktiv

12. PRODUCTION DEPLOY
================================================================================

PRODUCTION UCHUN TAYYORLASH:

  1. Settings o'zgartirish:
     ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
     │ DJANGO_SETTINGS_MODULE=config.settings.production              │
     │ DEBUG=False                                                     │
     │ SECRET_KEY=<kuchli-random-kalit-256-bit>                       │
     │ ALLOWED_HOSTS=yourdomain.com                                   │
     └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  2. Frontend build:
     ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
     │ cd wordfix-frontend                                             │
     │ npm run build                                                   │
     └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
     dist/ papkasida statik fayllar hosil bo'ladi.

  3. Gunicorn bilan ishlating (runserver o'rniga):
     ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
     │ gunicorn config.wsgi:application --bind 0.0.0.0:8000 \        │
     │   --workers 4 --timeout 120                                    │
     └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  4. Nginx sozlash (reverse proxy):
     - Frontend statik fayllar: dist/ dan serve qiling
     - Backend API: /api/ → http://localhost:8000
     - HTTPS uchun: Let's Encrypt sertifikat

  5. Xavfsizlik:
     - HTTPS majburiy (production.py da SECURE_SSL_REDIRECT=True)
     - HSTS yoqilgan (1 yil)
     - CORS faqat kerakli domainlar uchun
     - SECRET_KEY maxfiy saqlang
     - DB parolni kuchli qo'ying

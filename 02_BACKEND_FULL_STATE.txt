### SpacedRepetitionService (apps/words/domain/services/review_service.py — 186 qator)
- SM-2 Algorithm:
  - quality < 3: repetition=0, interval=1
  - quality >= 3: interval formula (1, 6, round(interval * EF))
  - EF = max(1.3, EF + (0.1 - (5-q)*(0.08+(5-q)*0.02)))
  - confidence = min(100, quality * 20)
  - is_mastered = repetition >= 5 AND confidence >= 80
